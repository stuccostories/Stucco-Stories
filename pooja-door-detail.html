The Pooja Door —
a small, stubborn piece of the past

Somewhere between a screen and a shrine, this door borrows a language older than the apartment around it. A woven teak jali, a scatter of brass bells, and a frame held together less by hardware and more by the sheer stubbornness of a cross-lap joint doing exactly what it was cut to do. The breakdown below follows the working drawing part by part — frame, shutter and the small stepped module that repeats until it reads as a pattern.

Opening: 3'-0″ × 7'-0″ Shutter Panel: 2'-8″ × 6'-0″ Module Pitch: 5″ × 5″ Material: Solid Teak
