02 / THE PRACTICE

Designed around
life as it happens.

Stucco Stories designs spaces around the lives unfolding within them. Based in Chennai, the independent architecture and interior design practice begins with how people move, gather, work and rest — shaping each space around the routines, needs and ambitions that make it personal.

Material and context guide every decision. Texture, craft and construction are considered alongside comfort, function and the way a surface will age. Plans respond to light, climate and the character of a place, allowing each project to develop its own identity.

Working across South India, Stucco Stories offers spatial planning for residential, commercial and institutional spaces, alongside interior design, renovations and design consultation. The aim is thoughtful, enduring design: spaces that support everyday activity, express the people who use them and feel natural to inhabit.

A WAY OF THINKING

Three things that
shape the work.

01

Begin with everyday life.

Observe the routines, movements and small rituals that make a space personal. Let the plan grow around them.

02

Let materials do their work.

Consider texture alongside performance, craft alongside use, and the character a material gains over time.

03

Respond to the place.

Work with the light, climate, surroundings and existing conditions. Each space deserves its own response.
